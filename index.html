FAQ

Foreign Worker Application FAQ

Short answers to the questions Malaysian employers ask us most often.

How do I apply for foreign workers in Malaysia?

Generally, employers first confirm eligibility for their sector, prepare company documents and complete the required employer applications, which may include Section 60K and FWCMS or FWe Approval-related steps, before worker recruitment and immigration processing. The exact steps depend on current government requirements. Final Choice can guide you through each stage.

Can my company apply for foreign workers?

It depends on your sector, business activity, company documents and current Malaysian government policies. Send us your company details and we will help you check which requirements may apply.

What documents are required?

Common documents include company registration documents, business licences, financial or tax documents, premise documents, worker records and sector-specific licences. Requirements vary by sector and application type, so ask us for a checklist for your company.

What is FWCMS?

FWCMS (Foreign Workers Centralized Management System) is an online system used for certain foreign worker-related applications and processing in Malaysia. Final Choice assists employers with preparing the information and documents needed for these submissions.

What is FWe Approval?

FWe Approval is an employer approval stage within the current foreign worker application process. Because procedures and requirements can change, we check the current process with you before preparing your documents.

What is Section 60K?

Section 60K of the Employment Act 1955 generally requires employers to obtain approval from the Labour Department before employing a foreign worker. Final Choice helps employers prepare this application and its supporting documents, where applicable.

Can Final Choice assist with foreign worker quota applications?

Yes. We assist employers with foreign worker quota applications, including document preparation and follow-up, subject to employer eligibility and current requirements. Approval decisions are made by the relevant authorities.

Which industries can hire foreign workers?

Foreign workers may generally be employed in approved sectors such as manufacturing, services, construction, plantation and agriculture. Eligible sub-sectors and job positions are subject to current Malaysian government policies.

How long does the application take?

There is no single fixed timeline. It depends on employer eligibility, sector, document completeness, government processing and any additional requests. We will explain the expected steps for your case, but processing times cannot be guaranteed.

Can you assist if I already started the application?

Yes. Tell us which stage your application is at and share what has been submitted. We will review it and advise on the next steps, where applicable.

Can you help prepare documents?

Yes. We help employers check, prepare and organise application documents and forms, and we let you know if anything is missing or needs correction.

Is Final Choice a licensed employment agency?

Yes. Agensi Pekerjaan Final Choice Sdn Bhd (201201028954 / 1013441-H) holds private employment agency licence JTKSM 483 (Category C). Our office is in Taman Mutiara Barat, Kuala Lumpur.

Where do I start if I have never applied before?

Start by sending us your company sector, business activity, job position, number of workers needed and company location. We will explain the likely requirements and the first steps for your company.
