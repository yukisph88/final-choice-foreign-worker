This Privacy Policy explains how Agensi Pekerjaan Final Choice Sdn Bhd (Company No. 201201028954 (1013441-H)) (“Final Choice”, “we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, discloses and protects personal data that you provide through this website and when you contact us by WhatsApp, telephone or email. It is issued in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Personal data we collect

When you make an enquiry, we may collect:

  • your full name, company name and job position;
  • your mobile / WhatsApp number and email address;
  • your company location and business sector;
  • details of your foreign worker requirements, such as the number of workers, job positions and the current stage of any application; and
  • any other information you choose to include in your message or share with us during the enquiry.

Please do not send copies of identity documents, passports or worker personal records through the enquiry form or in a first WhatsApp message. If such documents are needed for a service, we will tell you how to provide them. [CONFIRM: preferred secure method for receiving documents]

2. Obligatory and voluntary information

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) on the enquiry form — full name, company name, mobile / WhatsApp number, company location and business sector — are obligatory. If you do not provide them, we may not be able to respond to your enquiry. All other fields are voluntary.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal data directly from you when you fill in the enquiry form, message us on WhatsApp, call us, or email us.

At present, the enquiry form on this website does not store your details on our servers: it opens WhatsApp with your details prefilled, and your enquiry is only sent when you press Send in WhatsApp. [UPDATE THIS PARAGRAPH if an online form service is connected later]

4. Why we use your personal data

  • to respond to your enquiry and contact you about your foreign worker requirements;
  • to assess which services and application requirements may apply to your company, depending on employer eligibility and current requirements;
  • to provide the services you engage us for, and to communicate with you about them;
  • to keep records of our communications; and
  • to comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal data for marketing unrelated to your enquiry without your consent. [CONFIRM: whether Final Choice sends any marketing or newsletters]
